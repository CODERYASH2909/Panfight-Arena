HOW TO PLAY
The classic classroom pen fight, rebuilt with real physics.
1

Place Your Pen

Both pens start on opposite ends of the wooden bench. A 3-2-1-FIGHT countdown kicks off the duel.

2

Aim & Flick

Drag back on the canvas or use the angle & power sliders to flick. Mass, power, and friction decide trajectory.

3

Knock Them Off

Collide with your opponent's pen and send it sliding off the bench edge. Best-of-3 rounds win the match.

TURN-BASED PHYSICS COMBAT

PenFight Arena uses real momentum: mass, friction, angular velocity, and collision restitution decide what happens after every flick. Players alternate turns — once both pens come to a complete stop, the turn passes. Different pen archetypes (Heavy, Speed, Precision, Balanced) offer small, fair trade-offs — never a pay-to-win advantage. Pen Skins are purely cosmetic: colors, glows, and trail effects.
