▶ HOW IT WORKS
The classic classroom pen fight, rebuilt with real physics.
1️⃣

Place Your Pen

Both pens start on opposite ends of the bench. A 3-2-1-FIGHT countdown kicks things off.

2️⃣

Aim & Flick

Drag back from your pen to aim, release to flick. Power, direction, mass, and friction all matter.

3️⃣

Knock Them Off

Collide with your opponent's pen and send it sliding off the bench edge. Last pen standing wins.

Turn-Based Physics Combat

PenFight Arena uses real momentum: mass, friction, and collision physics decide what happens after every flick. Players alternate turns — once your pen (and your opponent's) comes to a complete stop, the turn passes. Different pen types (Heavy, Speed, Precision, Balanced) offer small, fair trade-offs — never a pay-to-win advantage. Pen Skins are purely cosmetic: colors, glow, and trail effects, nothing that changes gameplay.
